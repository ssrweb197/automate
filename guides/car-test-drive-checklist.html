A Practical Car Test-Drive Checklist for First-Time Buyers

What to inspect and test during a car test drive, from seating and visibility to braking, parking, controls and paperwork.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Independent buyer education

Before starting

Check that the seat, steering wheel and mirrors can be adjusted to a comfortable position. Make sure you can reach the pedals naturally and see the road clearly. Inspect the cabin for practical storage, rear-seat access and boot usability.

Drive on familiar road types

If the route permits, experience slow traffic, a rough patch, a speed breaker and a faster road. Notice ride comfort, cabin noise, steering effort, braking response and how smoothly the vehicle moves in traffic. Never exceed speed limits or perform unsafe manoeuvres during a test drive.

Try the features you will use

Check the infotainment interface, phone connectivity, air conditioning, parking sensors or camera, visibility and driver-assistance controls if fitted. Ask the representative to explain unfamiliar warning lights and safety features.

Evaluate passenger comfort

If possible, sit in the rear seat and ask regular passengers to do the same. Check headroom, legroom, seat support, door opening and child-seat installation requirements.

After the drive

Write down what felt comfortable and what did not while the experience is fresh. Confirm the exact variant, included equipment, warranty, delivery estimate and written price quotation. A test drive is one input into a decision, not a substitute for checking documentation.
