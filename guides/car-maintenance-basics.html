Basic Car Maintenance: A Simple Owner's Checklist

Learn the routine checks that help owners look after tyres, fluids, lights, brakes and service records, while knowing when to use a qualified technician.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Independent buyer education

Follow the owner's manual

Use the manufacturer's service schedule for your exact model, engine and model year. Service intervals and recommended fluids can differ. Keep invoices and service records in one place.

Check tyres regularly

Inspect tyre pressure when the tyres are cold and use the pressure specified by the manufacturer. Look for cuts, bulges, uneven wear and tread condition. Use the correct spare or repair equipment and seek professional help if a tyre is damaged.

Watch warning lights and fluids

Learn the meaning of dashboard warning lights from the manual. Check engine oil, coolant, brake fluid and washer fluid only as instructed and when safe. Never open a hot cooling system.

Check lights, wipers and brakes

Make sure headlamps, indicators, brake lamps and wipers work correctly. If you notice brake noise, vibration, pulling or a change in pedal feel, arrange a qualified inspection promptly.

Prepare for trips

Before a long journey, check tyres, lights, wipers, fluid levels, documents and emergency equipment. Maintenance guidance does not replace professional servicing or repairs.
