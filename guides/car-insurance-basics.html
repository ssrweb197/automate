Car Insurance Basics: IDV, Add-ons, Deductibles and Claims

A plain-language overview of common car insurance terms and questions to ask before choosing or renewing a policy.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Independent buyer education

Third-party and own-damage cover

Understand what each policy component covers and what it excludes. The legally required cover and optional protection are not the same. Check the current rules and policy wording for your vehicle.

Insured Declared Value

IDV is an important declared value used in certain total-loss or theft claim calculations, subject to policy terms. A lower premium is not automatically better if it comes with a materially different insured value or cover.

Deductibles and add-ons

Review compulsory and voluntary deductibles, and understand optional add-ons such as zero depreciation or roadside assistance where available. Add-ons have conditions, limits and exclusions; compare the wording rather than just the name.

Cashless garages and claims

Check the insurer's network garages in locations you actually use. Read the claim process, required documents, exclusions and timelines. Keep policy and vehicle details accurate.

Compare policies fairly

Compare the same IDV, coverage, deductibles, add-ons and claim conditions. Confirm the final premium and terms directly with the insurer or licensed intermediary. This guide is general information, not insurance advice.
