The Real Cost of Owning a Car: Beyond Fuel and EMI

A guide to estimating total car ownership costs, including depreciation, finance, insurance, service, tyres, parking and unexpected repairs.

Updated 14 November 2026 · Independent buyer education

Purchase and finance

Include the down payment, loan interest, processing charges and the full on-road amount. If you pay cash, remember that the money also has an opportunity cost.

Fuel or charging

Estimate monthly distance using your own routine. Divide by realistic efficiency and multiply by a current local fuel or electricity rate. Real-world efficiency varies with traffic, weather, load, driving style and vehicle condition.

Insurance and maintenance

Budget for insurance renewals, scheduled service, consumables, tyres, battery-related items where applicable and occasional repairs. Check the service schedule and warranty exclusions for the exact model.

Depreciation and resale

A vehicle usually loses value over time, but resale depends on condition, age, demand, kilometres, service history, location and market conditions. Treat resale estimates as uncertain rather than guaranteed.

Build a monthly ownership estimate

Add predictable monthly costs and set aside a reserve for irregular expenses. AutoMate's running-cost calculator can help estimate one part of the picture, but it does not automatically include every ownership cost.
